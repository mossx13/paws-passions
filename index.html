2. Rates

Drop-in $30. Per day (two visits) $50. Overnight (full day with feeding and overnight) $80. All visits include photos and video updates. After two pets total, add $15 per additional pet per day or night. Holiday sits add $20. Sits of a week or longer, or sits paid in full before service, receive 15% off. Preferred pay is half at the start date and half when you return. Balance is due no later than 48 hours after you return. Zelle, Apple Pay, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, and debit card (Stripe) are accepted. The app invoice deposit is one-half of the total. Bookings are non-refundable; cancel keeps the deposit.

You use the app at your own discretion. It is a booking and communication tool. You confirm you have the right to authorize care for every animal on the booking. Share house codes or a spare key set. Tell us if there is a security alarm or cameras.

Part II — Privacy Policy (Florida)

6. What we collect and why

We collect only what we need to talk with you about your pets and to run the sit: name, phone, email, address; pet name, age, gender, species/breed, medication, AM/PM feeding, notes; house codes or key notes; alarm and camera yes/no; emergency contact and veterinarian if you add them (we already have your phone and email, so we do not ask you to repeat those as the emergency person); dates, plan, fees, invoice, payment method and status; and your agreement to these terms.

We do not sell, rent, or broker your personal information. We do not use it to build unrelated advertising profiles. Florida’s Information Protection Act (Chapter 501, Florida Statutes) requires reasonable measures to protect personal information. We keep records on the booking device, our sit memory file, and with a payment processor if you pay by card. Stripe, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, Apple Pay, or PayPal see only what they need to move money. We do not store full debit or credit card numbers.

7. Who else may see it

Kaitlin Rogers and Anthony Barber (Tony), and a sitter acting for a booked sit, may see the file they need. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect a person or animal from immediate harm. Hosting the app may store technical logs. Those parties are not given your file to use for their own marketing.

8. How long we keep it and your choices

We keep a client file while we may sit for you again and for a reasonable time after the last sit for invoices and safety notes. You may ask what we have on file, correct it, or ask us to close a file we no longer need for a sit, taxes, or a legal claim. Call 860-709-7745 or email pawsandpassions@outlook.com. If we learn of a breach of personal information as Florida defines it, we will give notice as that law requires. The app is for adult pet owners. Do not submit a child’s personal information as if they were the client.

If these terms or this privacy policy change in a material way, we will post the new date in the app or tell you when you next book. Continued use after that date means you accept the update for future sits. A sit already booked keeps the deposit and cancel rules that applied when you submitted it, unless we both agree otherwise.
